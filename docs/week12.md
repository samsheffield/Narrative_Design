# Welcome

## Narrative Analysis Presentations
For this assignment, you will analyze the narrative design of a video game of your choice and present your findings to the class in a 5-10 minute presentation. __Don't forget to submit your preentations to the class Canvas.__

This presentation will include:
- A brief overview of the game (What is the title, who made it, when, what is the genre, what is the plot of the game's narrative?)
- 2-3 examples of narrative design decisions that you found compelling or successfully implemented in the game. Briefly describe how these choices were successful for this particular game through your experience as a player. 
- Include media: use still images or short embedded video clips that reinforce your talking points with specific, relevant moments from the game.

## Final Project
This is a freeform narrative assignment of your own choosing. It can be non-digital, interactive fiction, or something built in Unity/Unreal. You are welcome to revisit your first project and expand upon it in meaningful ways for this assignment. Collaboration is also welcome on this project, particularly if you want to experiment with integrating Ink with Unity.

### Project Deliverables:
The deliverables for this project are:
- Project pitch
- PDF of the outline for your game’s narrative
- Completed game uploaded to itch.io page (+ project files shared via Canvas)

### Project Pitch (Due by start of class on 11/20)
Describe your game in just a few sentences addressing the following points:
- What kind of game is it (examples: interactive fiction, 2D platformer, 3D exploration, etc.) and [what narrative genre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_writing_genres) does it belong to?
- What is the setting?
- Who is the player?
- What is the player's motivation or goals?
- How does the player accomplish these goals in the game?

#### Format
- Text document (pdf preferred). _We'll also go around the room and share ideas._

### Narrative Outline & Worldbuilding Documents (Due by end of class on 11/20)

#### Plot Summary & Outline
1. Draft a written plot summary for your game. _Who are we? Where are we? What happens?_
2. Outline your game on paper or using software like Milanote. The outline should identify __key events__, their __emotional beats__, and the __through-lines in your narrative__.

For each event you create in your outline:
- Write 1-2 sentences explaining what occurs at this moment. 
- Note the type of dramatic beat (according to the Hamlet’s Hit Points rubric from earlier this semester).

#### Worldbuilding Documents
1. Describe your world, what is in it, key relationships, and a brief timeline of its recent history. _Refer to [worldbuilding exercise Phase 1 document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1T0WtEgMmcVCvPVTR85dP5w79g6XZu5RAn-aM925B4Y0/edit?usp=sharing)._
2. Describe your main character(s). Who are they, what are their motivations, what do they look like. _Refer to [worldbuilding exercise Phase 2 document](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1zCtplxSHjDGH3anDY9o7RzFWWj9s3tE8SkfbEwlP8jc/edit?usp=sharing)._

#### Format
- Text documents (pdf preferred), digital images, and/or link to online moodboard uploaded to Canvas.
